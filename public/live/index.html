RESET Live · A small group programme · February 2027

You've tried everything to make it stop. But knowing your pattern and changing it are two different things.

You've talked it through, you journalled about it, you repeated affirmations that were supposed to help. You've had sessions where something landed and you thought, finally — and eleven weeks later you were doing the same thing with a different person.

So you did more. Another book, another practitioner, another six months of being extremely honest with yourself. You got better at describing what you do, and no better at not doing it.

That isn't because you've not tried hard enough or chose the wrong therapist. The decision underneath it was made long before you had words for anything, and words don't reach it. Everything you've tried has been aimed one level too high.

Changing it is a different job — and it is the one I have done with more than a thousand women over twelve years.

The programme

RESET Live is the method, run as a small group.

Eight weeks, one live session a week, from February 2027 — the same five stages and the same sequence I use one to one.

It uses three approaches, and the reason it works differently is that they run together rather than separately.

Coaching gets the conscious map: what you believe, what you want, what you can actually observe yourself doing. NLP gets the working parts: what sets it off, what it does, how it keeps proving itself right. Hypnotherapy goes to the level where the original decision was made, which is the only level where it can be changed.

Any one of these on its own gives you something real and then stops. You've probably met at least two of them already. Understanding without the mechanism leaves you articulate and stuck. The mechanism without the emotional layer gives you a better behaviour you can hold for about three months. The emotional work without a map leaves you moved and unsure what moved.

Used in sequence, each one prepares the next. You can't change a decision you can't reach, and you can't reach it without knowing what you're looking for. That sequence is the method — and it's the honest answer to why therapy helped and something still isn't finished.

The hypnosis is done live, with me, in the room. Not recorded. That's the part that only works live, and it's why this is a group and not a course.

Valentina Tudose

I created this method the hard way.

For years I could describe my own pattern perfectly. I was the capable one. I never asked, never let anyone see me struggle, didn't know how to ask for help. It almost killed me — I survived six days with a burst appendix rather than make a fuss. I tried my best to change it, and I did it anyway. Not once. For years, with full understanding.

What I didn't know was that I needed to find when I made the decision, and why. I had no memory of any moment that would justify it. Not repressed in any dramatic sense — just gone, the way most of these are, filed as unimportant by a child with no idea she was deciding something that would outlast everything.

At forty-two I retrieved that memory in a hypnotherapy session. A maths test at fourteen, a public failure, and a rule I wrote that afternoon about never being the one who needs. Nothing to do with love. The rule didn't care.

It didn't change because I could recognise the pattern. It changed when I could reach the moment it was made — and that's what the hypnosis is for.
